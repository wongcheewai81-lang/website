## Conditions Treated

Dr Chee Wai Wong provides specialist care for a wide range of retinal and vitreous conditions:

### Macular Conditions

- **Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD)** - treatment of wet and dry macular degeneration
- **Macular Holes** - full-thickness and lamellar macular hole repair
- **High Myopia Complications** - myopic macular degeneration and associated complications
- **Myopic Traction Macular Detachment** - management of structural macular changes in highly myopic eyes
- **Diabetic Macular Oedema** - treatment of macular swelling associated with diabetes

### Retinal Conditions

- **Retinal Detachment** - rhegmatogenous and tractional retinal detachment repair
- **Retinal Vein Occlusion** - central retinal vein occlusion and branch retinal vein occlusion management
- **Diabetic Retinopathy** - advanced diabetic eye disease management
- **Epiretinal Membranes** - surgical removal of scar tissue affecting the retina
- **Eye Floaters and Vitreous Conditions** - vitreous opacities and floater management

### IOL-Related Conditions

- **Dislocated Intraocular Lens (IOL)** - management of IOL decentration and malposition
- **IOL Exchange Surgery** - replacement of poorly positioned or damaged intraocular lenses
- **Scleral Fixation of IOLs** - surgical fixation of IOLs without capsular support

## Procedures and Treatments

Dr Wong performs a comprehensive range of advanced retinal and vitreous procedures:

### Surgical Procedures

- **Pars Plana Vitrectomy** - microsurgical removal of vitreous to treat retinal and vitreous pathology
- **Scleral Buckling** - external surgical technique to repair retinal detachment
- **Internal Limiting Membrane (ILM) Peeling** - removal of the ILM to facilitate macular hole closure and epiretinal membrane removal
- **Scleral Fixation of Intraocular Lenses** - surgical fixation technique for IOLs requiring alternative support
- **IOL Exchange Surgery** - removal and replacement of intraocular lenses
- **Cryotherapy** - cryogenic freezing technique used in retinal detachment surgery and other conditions

### Injection-Based Treatments

Dr Wong performs intravitreal injections of:

- **Anti-VEGF Agents:** ranibizumab, aflibercept, brolucizumab, and faricimab - for wet macular degeneration, diabetic macular oedema, retinal vein occlusion, and other neovascular conditions
- **Corticosteroid Injections:** for macular oedema and inflammatory retinal conditions

### Laser-Based Treatments

- **Photocoagulation** - laser treatment for diabetic retinopathy, retinal breaks, and other retinal conditions
- **Laser Retinopexy** - pneumatic retinopexy combined with laser to seal retinal breaks
- **Panretinal Photocoagulation (PRP)** - extensive laser treatment for advanced diabetic retinopathy and retinal vein occlusion
